I am a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering (AI & Systems) at **Rice University**, advised by [César A. Uribe](https://cauribe.rice.edu/). Previously, I received my B.S. in Statistics and Machine Learning with an additional major in Artificial Intelligence from **Carnegie Mellon University**.

My research lies at the intersection of **optimization, statistics, and machine learning**. I develop reliable computational methods for complex, high-dimensional data, with a focus on optimal transport, structured network inference, and the statistical evaluation of generative models. More broadly, I am interested in representation learning, generative modeling, and machine learning for scientific applications.

My recent work studies feasible Gromov–Wasserstein learning, sparse ecological network recovery under imperfect detection, and goodness-of-fit testing for time series. See my [research overview]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) and [full publication list]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
